# Weather Icons font

`weathericons-regular-webfont.ttf` is the **Weather Icons** font by Erik Flowers,
licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 — full text bundled alongside it in
[`OFL.txt`](OFL.txt) (also at https://openfontlicense.org).
Project: https://erikflowers.github.io/weather-icons/
Used here for the hourly wind-direction arrow glyphs and the per-hour readout icons
(raindrop, snowflake, humidity, sun, thermometer).
